The project

Bergamo, the memory of places

Bergamo, the memory of places is a progressive web app (PWA) created by students of the 5th IG class at ITIS P. Paleocapa in Bergamo (school year 2025/2026) to map 18 strategic locations in the city linked to the Fascist era and the Resistance. The site transforms a simple walk into an augmented learning experience: each place speaks through historical fact sheets, audio guides, period photographs and videos, available in Italian and English.

The model is scalable and replicable: the open-source code hosted on GitHub allows future classes and other schools to add new itineraries, passing the torch of collective memory year after year.

The prototype is available at itinerari-memoria.github.io .

How we worked

Methodology

The project adopts Project Based Learning (PBL): students do not receive concepts to memorize, but face a real problem — how to make the historical memory of their territory accessible — and solve it by collaborating with guided autonomy.

🗺️

Field research

On-site visits, meeting with author Mario Pelliccioli

✍️

Production

Historical fact sheets, bilingual texts, selection of archival photos and videos

💻

Web development

HTML, CSS, JS, Leaflet maps, QR scanner, GitHub Pages

🔍

Validation

Peer assessment, teacher mentoring, scientific validation by ISREC

📍

On-site deployment

Physical QR codes at the locations, event with the Municipality of Bergamo

What we want to achieve

The objectives

  • Bring to light stories of local figures linked to the Resistance, such as Ernesto and Ada Rossi and the Turani Band
  • Analyze places and events of Bergamo's fascism, connecting them to current issues
  • Develop linguistic mediation skills with bilingual Italian-English texts
  • Design a website that can be expanded in future years by other classes and schools
  • Place physical QR codes at the 18 locations, making the city "speak"
  • Promote Cooperative Learning, problem solving and peer evaluation
